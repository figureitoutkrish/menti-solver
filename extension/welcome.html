Set up Menti Solver

Three steps, about a minute. Your key stays in this browser.

  1. 1

    Get a free Groq key

    Sign in to Groq, open API Keys and create a key. It’s free and doesn’t need a card.

    Open Groq console
  2. 2

    Keys start with gsk_.

  3. 3

    Pin it and join a quiz

    Click the puzzle piece in Chrome’s toolbar, then the pin next to Menti Solver. Join any quiz on menti.com and it starts on its own.

    Open menti.com

Change keys, models and behaviour later in Settings.
